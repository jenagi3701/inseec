    HOW TO PLAY

    1 · 👀 LOOKThe bus stops. Passengers board. A free spot is marked with ★ and a bouncing arrow.
    2 · 🧠 THINKWho needs that spot most right now? Read the clues on each passenger.
    3 · ⚡ CLICKTap / click them before the timer runs out. Faster = more points. 3 mistakes = game over.

    🔁 Context matters. A wheelchair user needs the ♿ space, not a seat. A free priority seat (orange, striped, with a cane icon) should go to someone who needs it. When two people need it equally, either answer is right.

    ⚡ Speed bonus: <1s +100 · <2s +75 · <3s +50 · <5s +25. Combos at 3 / 5 / 10 multiply your points.

    ⌨ Keyboard: ← → or Tab to move, Enter/Space to pick, 1–9 to pick by number, P/Esc to pause.

    CLUE BOOK
